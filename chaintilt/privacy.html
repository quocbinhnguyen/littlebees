Chain Tilt Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Chain Tilt is a mobile game made by Little Bees, an independent studio run by Quoc Binh Nguyen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. You can play without creating an account. Your records then stay on your device.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your best scores, longest chains and settings (sound, music, vibration, control mode, tilt sensitivity, language) are saved on your device. We do not receive this data unless you join the online leaderboard.

Motion sensor readings are used only on your device to move the marble. They are never recorded or sent anywhere.

2. Online leaderboard (optional)

If you choose Join the Leaderboard, we create an anonymous guest account. No email or password is needed. We then store:

  • the nickname you choose, which is shown publicly on the leaderboard;
  • a random account ID, which is never shown to other players;
  • your results (score, longest chain, control mode, time of play, app version) and leaderboard rewards.

If you choose to link Google (Android) or Apple (iPhone) to protect your account, the sign-in provider shares an account identifier and, if available, your email address with us. We use them only to let you restore your account. We never show them to other players.

This data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

3. Notifications (optional)

Chain Tilt only sends notifications if you turn them on in the game and allow them on your phone. We then store a notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service that delivers the messages), your game language, your time-zone offset (not your location) and the date you last opened the game. We use them to send:

  • daily, weekly and monthly winner announcements, and a congratulation when you win;
  • if you have not linked an account, a reminder when you reach the top 10 so you can protect your records;
  • at most two reminders if you have not played for a while (after 7 and 30 days), sent around 7 pm your time.

Turn notifications off at any time in Settings → Player or in your phone's settings. Turning them off, signing out or deleting your account removes the token from our servers.

4. Game analytics

To understand how players use the game and improve it (for example, how many players come back the next day, how long runs last, and where players stop playing), Chain Tilt uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects:

  • anonymous game events, such as a run starting or ending, its score, duration and control mode, joining the leaderboard, sharing a result, and watching an ad;
  • an app instance ID created by Firebase, and basic device information such as device model, operating system version, language and approximate region (derived from your IP address);
  • app usage such as first open, sessions and time spent in the app.

We never send your nickname, email address, account ID or notification token to Analytics, and we do not use Analytics data for advertising. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage on your device is used only if you allow it in Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months after your last use of the game. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Advertising

Chain Tilt shows ads between runs, never during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), and ad interaction and diagnostic data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete in the game: Settings → Player → Delete Online Account permanently removes your online account, nickname, results and rewards. Records on your device stay.
  • Inactive guest accounts: a guest account that is not linked to Google or Apple may be deleted after a long period of inactivity. Before that, we will try to remind you in the game or by notification.
  • Request by email: if you can no longer open the game, email us at littlebees.app@gmail.com with your nickname, and we will delete your online account.

Leaderboard history is kept only as long as needed for rankings and rewards.

8. Children

Chain Tilt is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has joined the leaderboard, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
