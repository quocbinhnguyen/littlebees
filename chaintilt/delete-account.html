Delete your Chain Tilt account

Chain Tilt by Little Bees · Last updated: November 22, 2026

You only have an online account if you joined the leaderboard. If you never joined, there is nothing to delete online. Your records stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Chain Tilt and tap the Settings (gear) button.
  2. Under Player, tap your nickname to open Player Account.
  3. Tap Delete Online Account, then Delete Account to confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the game, email littlebees.app@gmail.com with the subject "Chain Tilt account deletion" and your nickname. If your account is linked to Google or Apple, send the email from that address so we can confirm it is yours. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your online account and account ID
  • Your nickname and every leaderboard result
  • Leaderboard rewards, such as ad-free time
  • The link to your Google or Apple account, if any

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Records and settings saved on your device are not affected. Advertising data is handled by Google; see the privacy policy.
